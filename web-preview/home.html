Why it's skill, not luck

Built so the better player wins.

Luck evens out over a match. What's left is positioning, risk and timing — and an engine that no one can rig.

🧠

Rating-based matchmaking

An ELO-style rating pairs you with opponents of similar skill, so results reflect play, not mismatches.

🎲

Certified provably-fair dice

Each roll is drawn from a pre-committed seed you can verify after the match. No server-side favours, ever.

🛡️

Server-authoritative engine

Game logic runs on our servers, not your device. Modified clients and auto-play bots can't change the board.

🕵️

Anti-collusion monitoring

Pattern detection flags chip-dumping and teaming in 4-player tables, with manual review on payouts.
